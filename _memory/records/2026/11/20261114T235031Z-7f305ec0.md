Goal g2-chord-core-roundtrip (G2): ChordPro parse and save round-trip, extended line model, transpose and capo in packages/chord-core. Outcome: shipped on branch feat/g2-chord-core-roundtrip (6 task commits a6a5ff4..7799770; Phase 5 audit APPROVED).

Delivered:
- **Line model.** A 10-kind SongLine union: lyric, chords, empty, meta, comment, section, tab, remark, directive and raw. It comes with Diagnostic/ParseResult types and a normative docs/CHORDPRO.md covering line classification, tags, diagnostics with exact messages, canonical form, an exhaustive normalization list, API, Unicode, transpose and capo.
- **Parser.** A hand-written line-oriented parseSong that never throws. One source line gives one SongLine, and chordsheetjs was removed from the workspace.
- **Serializer.** A canonical serializeSong. serialize(parse(x)) is canonical, idempotent and model-stable for 14 fixtures.
- **Chords.** parseChord, transposeChord and transposeKey with key-aware sharp/flat spelling (spec tables, ties default to sharp), plus transposeSong, which rewrites {key} and {new_key}, and viewSong, a view-only capo where shapes = sounding − capo.
- **Fixtures.** Six new original fixtures with hand-derived goldens, canonical and diagnostics files. The 8 G1 goldens were extended additively.
- **chord-render.** SongView renders every kind through an exhaustive switch.
- **Tests.** Closer round-trip and transpose tests, plus a G2 docs↔model contract test.

Key decisions:
- Canonical rather than byte-identical save.
- Own parser, after whole-song ChordSheetJS parsing proved lossy.
- Remarks and directives are kept in `lines`, and goldens are regenerated additively.
- Kit-owned text that still mentions ChordSheetJS is left for an agent-kit re-emit.
